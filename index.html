Format

How It Works

  1. 1
    Friday: Group 1

    Group 1 plays 7 games. The top 4 contestants advance to the finals on Sunday.

  2. 2
    Saturday: Group 2

    We repeat the same for Group 2: 7 games, top 4 advance to the finals.

  3. 3
    Sunday: Finals, check format

    Players that reach 30 points put the lobby in check. The first of these players to win a lobby wins the tournament! You need 30 points at the start of a game, so reaching 30 while winning a lobby doesn't count.

  4. 4
    Finals scoring

    Placement points remain largely the same during the finals, but first place is awarded 10 points and second place 8.
